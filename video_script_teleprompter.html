Part 1: The Problem & Introduction 0:00 – 0:25
[Keep screen showing two browser windows side by side]

"Hello judges. I am Venkatesan, and along with my teammate Sasmita, we are Team Hexcore submitting for the Open Innovation track.

In every residential neighbourhood, urgent needs happen daily: an elderly resident running out of heart medicine in the rain, or a parent stranded at home with a sick toddler needing baby formula.

Today, people post these into chaotic WhatsApp or Telegram groups where requests get buried in seconds. Nobody knows who is helping whom, and volunteers hesitate to share their personal phone numbers with hundreds of strangers.

We built Relay: a dispatch-first community chat powered by the Nexconn Chat SDK."

Part 3: Volunteer Live Feed & The Claim Flow 0:55 – 1:25
[Move cursor to Right Window: Venkatesan / Volunteer]

"Over on the volunteer's screen, the ticket appears live in the community feed with an urgent pulsing red badge.

[Point cursor to the ticket in feed]

The volunteer can review the description, urgency, and distance. When ready, he clicks 'Claim & Chat'.

[Click 'Claim & Chat →']

Instantly, Relay provisions a dedicated, private chat room powered by Nexconn's DirectChannel infrastructure. Notice that the chat room opened simultaneously on both screens. Neither person had to expose their personal phone number or social media."

Part 4: Real-Time Chat, Typing Indicator & Quick Chips 1:25 – 1:55
[In Right Window: start typing a message]

"Now, both neighbours can coordinate in real time. Notice on Eleanor's screen on the left: Nexconn's live typing indicator appears, letting her know the volunteer is actively responding.

[Click the quick-reply chip 'On my way now' and hit send]

The volunteer taps the 'On my way now' quick chip to save time while driving. Inbound messages are delivered instantly over low-latency WebSockets. We can also attach photos of prescription receipts or drop-off locations."

Part 5: Status Lifecycle Stepper & Thank-You Rating 1:55 – 2:20
[In Right Window: Click 'Start Task']

"At the top of the room is our four-stage lifecycle banner: Open, Claimed, In Progress, and Resolved. When the volunteer reaches the pharmacy, he marks it 'In Progress', updating both screens.

[In Right Window: Click 'Mark Resolved', type: 'Medicine delivered safely to door', and confirm]

Once delivered, he marks it 'Resolved'.

[In Left Window: Click 'Rate & Thank', select 5 stars, type: 'Thank you so much!', and submit]

Eleanor is prompted to leave a five-star thank-you review, triggering a celebration and archiving the verified outcome into the neighbourhood record."
